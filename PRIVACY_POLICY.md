Privacy Policy — Discord Voucher Bot

Last Updated: October 26, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to the Discord Voucher Bot, developed and maintained by Atsu. This Privacy Policy explains how the bot collects, uses, and protects information when users interact with its features.

2. Information We Collect

The bot may process the following information when users interact with it:

- Discord User ID: To identify users who submit vouchers.
- Discord Username: To display the identity of users who submit or receive vouchers.
- Voucher Information: Ratings, reviews, and comments submitted through the "/vouch" command.
- Server Information: Server IDs and related information necessary for the bot to operate.

The information actually stored depends on the bot's implementation.

3. How We Use Your Information

Information is used only to:

- Process and display voucher submissions.
- Identify users involved in voucher transactions or reviews.
- Maintain the bot's functionality.
- Troubleshoot technical issues and prevent abuse.

4. Data Storage and Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information handled by the bot. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

Data storage and retention depend on the bot's hosting environment and implementation.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. Information may be visible to server members when voucher submissions are posted publicly in Discord.

We do not intentionally share collected information with unrelated third parties, except where necessary to operate the service or comply with applicable law.

6. Data Retention and Deletion

Voucher information may be retained for as long as necessary to provide the bot's features.

Users who want their stored voucher information deleted may contact the developer, Atsu, through the bot's official support server or another designated support channel.

7. Third-Party Services

The bot operates through Discord and may use Replit or other hosting services. These services may process information as necessary to provide their respective services and are subject to their own policies.

8. Children's Privacy

Users must comply with Discord's minimum age requirements and all applicable laws.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's features or data practices change. The latest version will be made publicly available.
